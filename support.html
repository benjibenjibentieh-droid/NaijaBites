Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add meals to your cart, choose Proceed to Order and complete the demo checkout form. No payment or delivery is actually arranged.

Where can I find my orders?

Use the My Orders shortcut on the marketplace to view orders saved in this browser.

Are table reservations real?

No. Reservations are demo records stored locally and are not sent to restaurants.

How do I update my profile?

Open your profile from the marketplace and choose Edit Profile. This demo stores profile data in your browser.

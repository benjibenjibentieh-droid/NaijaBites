WHO WE ARE

A marketplace with a taste of home.

Food is more than something we eat. It brings families together, celebrates traditions and creates lasting memories.

NaijaBites is a food marketplace designed to help customers explore meals from local vendors in one convenient place. From everyday favourites to special dishes, we want everyone to discover food they love.

We also want local food businesses to become easier to find, helping them reach customers who appreciate their cooking.
